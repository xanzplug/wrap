One project, start to finish

Stop jumping between
five different apps.

Most tools only handle the final file. Wrap keeps the whole project around it: what you planned to shoot, what you edit with, and what you sent.

01Plan

A shot list you'll actually use on set.

Add the shots you need with reference photos and notes. Start from a template, set a shoot date, and tick shots off as you get them.

  • Reference image on every shot
  • Reusable shot list templates
  • Reminders the evening before and two hours before a shoot

02Edit

Your whole editing setup, one click.

Add your editor, the project file, the footage folder and your music to the project's workspace. Press Launch and everything opens together.

  • Add apps, files, folders and websites
  • Launch from the dashboard or the menu bar
  • Hide it all again in one click when you're done

03Deliver

Send the final cut with a link.

Drop the finished file into the project. Wrap uploads it, copies a download link, and tells you when your client grabs it. The link expires on its own.

  • Links last 1 day, 2 days or a week
  • A notification when the client downloads
  • Big files upload in parts and retry if the connection drops

First time opening it?
  1. Unzip the download and drag Wrap into your Applications folder.
  2. Open it. If macOS says it can't check the app, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  3. Sign in or make a free account. After that, Wrap updates itself.
